About Me

Welcome to my webpage!

I am Associate (Tenured) Professor of Economics at IE University School of Politics, Economics and Global Affairs (SPEGA), Madrid. I am also a Research Fellow at IZA&LISER and a member of the International Health Economics Association.

I am an empirical microeconomist working on the economics of the family, gender, health, and public policy evaluation. I combine administrative data and large-scale survey data to analyze the impact of public policies on gender outcomes — with a particular focus on labor market behaviour, health, marriage, and fertility decisions. I am also interested in how policies and social contexts shape gender norms and their effect on marriage, fertility, and well-being.
